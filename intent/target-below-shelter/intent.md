# Target below shelter

Status: done
Opened: 29 October 2026 by Paul Newell

## Problem

The segment target panel sits between the timeline and the ride cockpit, ahead of the details it depends on. Its placeholder, 1:30, reads like a suggested time rather than a format example.

## Proposed outcome

The segment target panel sits directly below the assumed shelter panel. Its placeholder reads 1:00.

## Affected users and parts

Every rider viewing a saved segment. `apps/web/app/routes/wind-preview.tsx`, `target-settings.tsx`, their ARIA baselines, `docs/design.md`.

## Constraints

No change to behaviour, storage, parsing or requests. ARIA baselines are regenerated by the test runner.
