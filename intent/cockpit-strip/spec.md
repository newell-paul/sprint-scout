# Spec: Cockpit fact strip

## Behaviour

- **Desktop:** the cockpit shows the speed dial and the wind compass side by side in equal columns. Under them, spanning both, a strip shows:
  - the difference from still air ("6s slower than still air"), coloured by the cockpit tone as before;
  - still-air time (or "Still air · same terrain"), estimated range, segment distance and, under shelter, the exposed comparison, each as a muted label and its value on one line;
  - the modelling note and the shelter note in small muted text;
  - the target comparison when a target is set, reading "Compare with the estimated range; not a success probability."
- **Unavailable:** when there is no effect, the strip shows the existing message (wind missing, terrain not loaded, or cannot be modelled) in place of the facts.
- **Segment time:** the cockpit no longer shows the large time. The chart's "Selected" figure is the segment time; it takes the `segment-time-value` test id.
- **Compass:** the wind speed reads in the centre of the ring ("Calm" or "Unavailable" when there is none), the arrow sits on the ring's edge pointing where the wind blows, and "Towards …" with the wind character goes underneath. The reading scales with the ring so it stays inside the ticks at every width. The crosshair goes.
- **Phone (600 px and below):** the dials stay side by side with smaller drawings, and the strip wraps under them.
- The cockpit remains the "Ride instruments" region with `aria-live="polite"`. The strip has no heading.

## Data and privacy

No change.

## Calculations

No change. Every value comes from the same `RouteRideEffect`.

## Tests

- **Component:** `ride-cockpit.browser.test.tsx`: the ready case checks the still-air difference instead of the large time; the wind-missing count of "Unavailable" drops from 4 to 3. The `ride-cockpit.*` and `wind-preview.*` ARIA snapshots are regenerated by the runner.
- **E2E:** `e2e/pages/cockpit.ts` keeps `segmentTime` on the `segment-time-value` test id, which now sits on the chart. The `cockpit-tailwind` ARIA snapshot is regenerated. The visual baselines that include the cockpit or the compass (`dial-tailwind`, `dial-calm`, `compass-southerly` and any others that fail) are regenerated in the Linux container.
