# Add segment flow: spec

From the accepted [intent](intent.md).

## Behaviour

**Drawing mode** is any time the selected slot has no saved segment, or a saved segment is being redrawn. It is the same screen as today, laid out again.

**The bar.** A `drawing-bar` sits directly above the map in drawing mode, in place of the empty-slot hint, the "Drawing progress" step list and the guidance line. It holds:

- A live text (`aria-live="polite"`):
  - 0 points: "Tap the map to place your start."
  - 1 point: "Now tap the finish. Lines go straight between points."
  - 2 or more: "Tap to add bends, then save below."
- "Undo" (accessible name "Undo point"), disabled with no points.
- "Cancel" (accessible name "Cancel drawing"), shown when there is something to cancel: a draft (points, a typed name, or a redraw), or a saved segment to go back to. With an empty library and nothing drawn there is nowhere to go, so it is hidden.

On a phone the text wraps above the two buttons when it does not fit on one line.

**Cancel and Escape.** Cancel, and Escape pressed anywhere on the page in drawing mode, do the same thing:

- During a redraw: drop the edits and show the saved segment.
- On a new segment: drop the drawing and go back to the segment the rider came from. If that slot is now empty, go to the lowest saved slot. With no saved segments, stay on the empty slot.
- If there was a draft, show the discard notice. If not (a new segment with nothing drawn or typed), just go back.

**No lock.** The segment tabs stay enabled in drawing mode. Picking another tab while there is a draft discards it, as Cancel does, then selects that tab. The "Save or cancel your drawing before switching segments." line goes.

**Discard notice.** "Drawing discarded." with "Restore drawing" and "Dismiss", styled like the delete notice. Restore puts back the slot, points, name, tick and redraw target exactly as they were. The notice is hidden while another draft is in progress, and cleared by a save, by Dismiss or by a later discard. The status line reads "Drawing discarded. Saved segments are unchanged." on discard and "Drawing restored." on restore.

**First tap.** In drawing mode the click or tap that activates the map also places a point. On a saved segment no tap places a point, as before. The resting-mouse hint "Click to use the map" stays.

**Name.** A new segment's name field shows "Segment N" (N is the slot number the rider sees) until the rider types. Focusing the field while it still shows the default selects it, so typing replaces it. Clearing the field to nothing blocks Save with "Enter a segment name to save your segment." A redraw starts with the saved name. Only typed text counts as a draft.

**Editor order** under the map: the point count line, "Segment name", the tick (wording unchanged), "Save segment" with its reason line, "Enter coordinates instead", then "Ordered route points" holding "Reverse direction" above the list. "Clear drawing" is removed.

**Wording.** "Save route" becomes "Save segment", "Route name" becomes "Segment name", "Route saved on this browser." becomes "Segment saved on this browser.", and the save reasons say "segment". "Editing X. Your saved version stays unchanged until Save." stays.

## Data and privacy

No new network request, no new storage. The discard notice holds the dropped draft in memory only; a reload loses it.

## Calculations

None.

## Tests

E2e, in `e2e/drawing.spec.ts` unless noted:

- The first click on a new segment's map places the start (replaces "the click that hands the map control places no point").
- The bar's text follows the drawing: start, finish, bends.
- Cancel on a new segment with nothing drawn goes back to the segment the rider came from, with no discard notice.
- Cancel after drawing goes back and offers Restore; Restore brings back the points and the slot.
- Escape cancels a drawing.
- Picking another tab while drawing discards the drawing and opens that tab; Restore brings it back (replaces "the other slots are locked during a redraw").
- A new segment saves under its default name.
- With an empty library and nothing drawn, there is no Cancel.
- Existing tests move to the new names: the save reason text, "Segment saved on this browser." in the page object and `keyboard.spec.ts`, "Ordered route points" opening before Reverse.
- The two Clear drawing tests go with the button. Undo still covers removing points; the "a cleared draft that is never saved leaves the segment in place" case becomes the same check after Cancel.
- `library.emptyMessage` is replaced by the bar text.
- The `drawing-ready-to-save` ARIA baseline is regenerated by the runner.
